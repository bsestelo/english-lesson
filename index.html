Parent guide

Goal: help your child connect a picture and spoken English word with her own attempt at writing three letters.

Flow: picture → hear the complete English word → repeat/listen again → write three letters → press I wrote it! → use Show book spelling.

Do not require conventional spelling on the first attempt. If she writes KET for CAT, you can say: “You listened and wrote three letters.” Then compare her version with CAT without asking her to erase it.

There are intentionally no isolated sound buttons. The activity uses only the complete spoken word.

Each lesson selects exactly 6 unique words. A new lesson avoids the six words used immediately before it.
